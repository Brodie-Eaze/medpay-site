The problem

Patients rarely say no to care. They say no to paying for it today.

The clinical case is made. The plan is agreed. Then the number lands and the conversation stops. That happens at the front desk of every practice, and it is the single largest source of unstarted treatment in the industry.

The product, in your hands

One application.
A clearer path to care.

This is the whole product. Your patient applies once. MedPay routes that one application across a panel that spans the full credit spectrum. Each lender answers for itself and the offers come back in one view.

The lender panel

Four tiers, so fewer patients hit a dead end.

A single lender declines and the conversation is over. A panel does not work that way. The same application is assessed across four tiers at once, so a patient who fails prime is still matched further down instead of being turned away.

Prime

Strongest credit profiles. Lowest rates and the lowest monthly cost on the same plan.

Near prime

Good but imperfect files. The tier that catches most patients a single prime lender would decline.

Specialty

Thinner or damaged files assessed on criteria a prime lender does not use.

Second look

The final tier. It exists so a decline higher up the panel is not the end of the treatment.

What matching is and is not. MedPay matches a patient to the lenders whose criteria they fit. MedPay does not score the patient, set the rate or make the decision. Each lender underwrites its own offer and owns its own disclosures.

The standard

Built like a finance company. Because it is one.

Every surface honours the rules that govern consumer credit. That is not overhead. That is the product.

Credit reporting done right

Pre-qualification runs on soft credit inquiries under FCRA. Hard pulls happen only on the lender's own surface and only after explicit patient consent.

Disclosures where they belong

APR and payment schedules are presented in the lender's Truth in Lending disclosure. We never paraphrase credit terms on our own screens.

Health data stays clinical

We never store patient PHI. Applications capture financial and identity data only. Clinical records stay in your practice management system and never enter the financing pipeline.

The lender decides

MedPay does not score, approve or decline. Every credit decision and every adverse action belongs to the lender that made it.

Is MedPay a lender?

No. MedPay is not a lender and does not make credit decisions. We route a single application to a panel of patient-financing lenders. Each lender underwrites on its own criteria and presents its own offer with full disclosures.

Does pre-qualification affect a patient's credit score?

No. Pre-qualification uses a soft credit check with no impact on the patient's score. A hard inquiry can only happen later on the lender's own application and only with the patient's explicit consent.

Does MedPay handle patient health information?

No. We never store patient PHI. The application captures financial and identity data only. Clinical information stays in your practice management system and is never part of the financing pipeline. MedPay is not a HIPAA covered entity.

How long does setup take?

One integration with your front desk. Most setups go live within five business days and front-desk training takes about thirty minutes. There is no rip and replace of your practice management system.
